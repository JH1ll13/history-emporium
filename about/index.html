About the Emporium

Learning history should feel like play

Mr. Teacher's History Emporium is a free collection of world history games for middle school students. It covers the last five hundred years, from the Renaissance to World War II, with a final challenge, History Hero, that brings the whole year together.

Each game is built around the research on how memory works: short, frequent questions spaced out over time, mixed together and tied to the story of each era. The goal is simple: students who remember the basics automatically have room to think about the big questions.

Safe for school

  • No chat, no user uploads and no links to outside sites.
  • No accounts and no personal information collected.
  • Everything loads from this site.
