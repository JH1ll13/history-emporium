Rift Runner

Sprint across neon rooftops in the rain. Jump gaps, slide under bars, dash through energy walls and chase your best distance.

What's inside

Obstacles that get trickier the farther you go, magnet, shield and double-coin power-ups, and 6 runners to unlock with coins.

How to play

Space or up arrow to jump (twice for a double jump), down arrow to slide, right arrow or Shift to dash. On a touchscreen, tap to jump, swipe down to slide and swipe right to dash.

Game type

Endless Runner
