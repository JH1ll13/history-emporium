EMPIRE CHASE

Age of Imperialism · 1757–1914

You play the Chronicler — a reporter racing across the map to record the facts while rival empires chase you.

How to play

  • 🟡 Move with arrow keys / WASD, swipe the map, or use the on-screen pad.
  • ✨ Eat every fact dot to clear the level.
  • ⭐ Grab a Source Star to answer a quick question. Right answer = power up — the empires flee and you can catch them!
  • ⚡ Answer in under 3 seconds for a Lightning bonus. Right answers in a row build a streak multiplier up to ×5.
  • 🎯 Find the place in the mission banner. Each find freezes the empires for a moment.
  • 🧠 Every level ends with a Recall Check that mixes in terms from earlier levels.
  • 🏆 Three stars per level: clear it, score 80%+, or go perfect and find every place.
