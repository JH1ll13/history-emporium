Dungeon Dash

Clear pixel-art dungeon rooms, pick from random power-ups and build a ridiculous combo before you face the Bone King.

What's inside

20 power-ups from common to legendary, a boss every 5 rooms, and gold that buys permanent upgrades between runs.

How to play

WASD or arrow keys to move, Space to dash. On a touchscreen, drag anywhere to move and tap DASH. Your knight aims and shoots on his own.

Game type

Roguelike
