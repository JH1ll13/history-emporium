Tower Titans

Place fire, ice, rock and lightning creatures along the path. They level up as they fight and evolve into Titans. Survive 25 waves!

What's inside

4 creature types with 3 evolutions each, a boss every 5 waves and the Gigaclops King at wave 25. Mix your elements to win.

How to play

Pick a creature at the bottom, then tap or click the grass to place it. Tap a creature to evolve or sell it.
